# qarabazar.az Real Estate Market Analysis
## Executive Business Intelligence Report

**Analysis Date:** January 1, 2026

**Data Source:** qarabazar.az (25 pages, 625 validated listings)

**Market Coverage:** Bakı and surrounding regions

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis provides actionable intelligence on the Azerbaijan real estate market based on 625 active listings. The market shows strong agent dominance (88% of listings), with houses being the most common property type. Pricing exhibits significant variance, with a median of 115,000 AZN versus an average of 371,474 AZN, indicating a market with both affordable entry points and premium opportunities.

### Key Market Metrics
- **Total Active Listings:** 625
- **Average Price:** 371,474 AZN
- **Median Price:** 115,000 AZN *(68% below average - opportunity indicator)*
- **Price Range:** 1,000 - 10,000,000 AZN
- **Market Composition:** 88% Agent-listed, 12% Owner-listed
- **Geographic Focus:** Bakı (primary market)

## Critical Business Insights

### Market Structure Findings

**1. Agent-Dominated Market (88%)**
- **Implication:** Professional intermediaries control access to inventory
- **Action:** Build strategic agent partnerships; consider in-house brokerage
- **Risk:** High agency fees may compress margins for buyers/sellers

**2. High Price Variance (Median 69% below Average)**
- **Implication:** Market has both volume (affordable) and value (premium) segments
- **Action:** Deploy tier-specific strategies; avoid one-size-fits-all approach
- **Opportunity:** Outlier properties may be mispriced - investigate individually

**3. House-Dominant Inventory**
- **Implication:** If apartments are in lower supply but high demand, there's opportunity
- **Action:** Prioritize apartment development if per-sqm pricing is favorable
- **Risk:** Oversupply of houses could indicate market saturation

### Geographic Concentration

**Bakı as Primary Market:**
- **Strength:** Deep liquidity, proven demand, infrastructure support
- **Challenge:** High competition, potentially inflated prices
- **Secondary Markets:** Lower volume regions may offer expansion opportunities with less competition

### Transaction Velocity Indicators

**Rental Market Presence:**
- If rentals constitute >20% of market: strong investor appetite for cash-flowing assets
- Build-to-rent strategies become viable
- Consider multi-family developments optimized for rental operations

## Data Quality & Methodology Notes

**Data Source:** qarabazar.az public listings (scraped January 1, 2026)
**Sample Size:** 625 validated listings (outliers removed for statistical integrity)
**Geographic Coverage:** Nationwide, with concentration in Bakı region
**Time Period:** Active listings as of analysis date (snapshot, not time-series)

**Data Cleaning Applied:**
- Removed listings with prices <1,000 AZN or >10,000,000 AZN (likely data errors)
- Excluded entries with missing critical fields (price, location)
- Normalized text fields for consistent categorization

**Limitations:**
- Snapshot data does not capture seasonal trends or time-series movements
- Listing prices may not reflect actual transaction prices (negotiation not captured)
- Sample represents active inventory, not completed sales
- Agent vs owner categorization based on listing metadata
